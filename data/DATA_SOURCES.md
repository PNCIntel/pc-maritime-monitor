# Data source notes

This build uses user-supplied datasets as its evidentiary basis.

- UKMTO warning classifications and summaries: supplied 2026 UKMTO source log.
- Port incidents: supplied updated 2026 Global Port & Terminal Incidents workbook.
- Maritime attacks and piracy: supplied updated 2026 maritime attacks/piracy master workbook.
- Black Sea: supplied BlackSeaNews maritime database extraction.
- Port investment: supplied 2026 Global Port Investment & Commercial Opportunities workbook.
- IUU structural risk: supplied IUU Fishing Risk Index 2019–2025 dataset.
- Sanctions/watchlists: supplied vessel blacklist/watchlist aggregator CSV.
- ReCAAP: previously normalized from supplied 2024–2026 annual incident PDFs.

EU official consolidated sanctions data is intentionally not a separate dependency in this build.

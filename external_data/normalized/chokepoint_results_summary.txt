
=== TVP-SVAR EMPIRICAL RESULTS SUMMARY ===

1. DATA & PREPROCESSING
   - Sample: 2014-01 to 2025-12, 143 monthly observations
   - Variables: ΔCCFI (shipping cost shock), HYZS (shipping stock returns), 
                ER (exchange rate volatility), OIL (oil price volatility)
   - All variables stationary at 5% level (ADF tests)

2. MODEL SPECIFICATION
   - TVP-SVAR(1) estimated via Kalman Filter + EM algorithm
   - Time-varying coefficients capture structural evolution
   - Variable ordering: ΔCCFI → HYZS → ER → OIL

3. KEY EMPIRICAL FINDINGS

   A) Time-Varying Coefficients:
   - ΔCCFI persistence increased from 2014 to 2022, then declined
   - HYZS persistence declined from 2015 to 2020, then partially recovered
   - ER persistence peaked during 2018 trade war period
   - Cross-market coefficients exhibit significant time variation

   B) Impulse Response Functions:
   - ΔCCFI → HYZS: Effect strongest in 2020-2022 period, weakens afterward
   - HYZS → ΔCCFI: Generally weak, inconsistent sign
   - ER → OIL: Positive effect, strongest during 2020-2021
   - All shocks decay to zero within 6-10 months

   C) Variance Decomposition (10-month horizon):
   - ΔCCFI: 79-99% explained by own shocks, HYZS contributes 0.2-8.6%
   - HYZS: 84-98% own shocks, ΔCCFI contributes 0.07-88.4% (time-varying)
   - ER: 60-90% own shocks, OIL contributes 0.8-29.3%
   - OIL: 53-96% own shocks, ER contributes 1.0-29.3%

   D) Key Insight: Cross-market transmission is TIME-VARYING
   - Pre-2020: Market segmentation relatively high
   - 2020-2022 (COVID): Cross-market linkages strengthened
   - Post-2022: Partial return to pre-COVID patterns but with higher persistence
